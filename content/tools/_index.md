<h2 class="section-heading">Academic Research</h2>

[**Academic Journal Monitor**](/tools/academic-journal-monitor/) — Live tracker of new articles across 70+ social-science journals — sociology, criminology, political economy, social theory, anthropology, political science — plus keyword discovery searches, powered by OpenAlex.

<h2 class="section-heading">Scholarly Writing</h2>

[**Ibid**](/ibid/) — Checks every citation in a manuscript against its bibliography, and every entry against the text: missing works, uncited entries, wrong years, and misspelled authors, in the text or in footnotes. For Mac (free beta).

[**Wordrange**](/wordrange/) — Counts the words in a PDF or Word manuscript part by part, the way journals count them: main text and notes, with or without the abstract, references, and appendices. For Mac (free).
